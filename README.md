# Petrie Daily Brief Reader

A reading interface for the **Petrie Institute’s AI intelligence briefs**, created and maintained by **grAIce tech**.

[Open the reader](https://petrie.graice.tech) · [Visit Petrie Institute](https://www.petrieai.org/) · [Receive the Institute’s newsletter](https://www.petrieai.org/newsletter) · [Contact the Institute](https://www.petrieai.org/)

## Purpose

AI developments affect strategy across markets, national security, governance, and research. The reader brings the Institute’s brief material into a simple interface organized by date and topic, helping scholars, military professionals, and executives revisit developments and follow their sources.

The Institute supplies the briefs and their editorial analysis. grAIce tech supplies the reader’s design, development, and maintenance.
